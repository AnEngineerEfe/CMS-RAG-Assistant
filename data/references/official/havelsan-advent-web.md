# ADVENT Combat Management System

HAVELSAN describes ADVENT as a network-supported, data-integrated Combat Management
System introduced in 2010 for force-oriented and network-centric naval operations. It
was developed with the Turkish Naval Forces and is intended to synchronize and use the
capabilities of all platforms participating in a mission rather than optimizing only a
single ship.

The current official product page lists enhanced situational awareness, network-enabled
services, joint engagement and training, intelligence-data integration, scalability
from small boats to command centres, and integrated Tactical Data Links including
Link 11, Link 16, Link 22, SIMPLE, JREAP and VMF. It also states compliance with NATO
standards.

ADVENT ROTA is the tailored unmanned-systems variant and supports network-enabled joint
missions with ADVENT-equipped systems. ADVENT UFUK is presented as a command-control
and intelligence/information-management solution for maritime security and maritime
situational awareness; it gathers and fuses data from coastal sensors to build a
recognized maritime picture. ADVENT MARTI is designed for special-mission aircraft and
helicopters and supports intelligence, reconnaissance, surveillance, situational
awareness and command-control functions.
