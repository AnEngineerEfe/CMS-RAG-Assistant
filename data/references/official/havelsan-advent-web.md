# ADVENT Combat Management System

HAVELSAN describes ADVENT as a network-supported, data-integrated combat management
system developed for force-oriented and network-centric naval operations. The system
is designed to synchronize the capabilities of platforms participating in a mission
rather than optimizing only one ship. The official product page highlights situational
awareness, network-enabled services, joint engagement and training, intelligence-data
integration, NATO-compatible interfaces, sensor fusion, flexible integration and work
with unmanned systems. The page also presents ADVENT UFUK as a command-control and
information-management solution for maritime security and maritime situational awareness.
