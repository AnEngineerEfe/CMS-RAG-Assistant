# NATO Digital Transformation and Interoperability

NATO's public Digital Transformation Implementation Strategy links multi-domain
operations to a digital backbone that supports seamless command and control across
domains. It identifies enhanced situational awareness, orchestration of operational
effects, risk management, digital mission assurance, data aggregation, and secure
interconnection as foundational elements. Interoperability requires data-centric
systems that can integrate securely across organizational and national boundaries.
This public reference provides general C2 and interoperability context; it does not
define or verify product-specific ADVENT capabilities.
