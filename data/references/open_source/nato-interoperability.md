# NATO Digital Transformation and Interoperability

NATO's public Digital Transformation Implementation Strategy 2.0 describes a secure,
interoperable and data-driven digital future intended to support multi-domain
operational effectiveness. Its Digital Backbone is a federation of networks and
systems that connects sensors, decision makers, actors and effectors across
organizational, national, operational and security-domain boundaries.

The strategy also defines an Alliance Data Sharing Ecosystem in which Allies, NATO
entities and trusted actors share, label and exploit interoperable data to accelerate
missions, improve efficiency and support decision-making. This ecosystem is governed
by an Alliance-wide data-centric framework that respects data sovereignty and national
regulations. Digital interoperability requires common regulatory, implementation and
technical enablers across NATO capabilities.

This public reference supplies general digital, C2-adjacent and interoperability
context. It does not define, certify or verify product-specific ADVENT capabilities.
